ABOUT THE PROGRAMME

The year is 2031. The first permanent human settlement on the moon is operational. It will eventually become home to everyone.

Broadcasting from Selene Base Alpha, the host of Lunar Law presents pre-recorded submissions from people across Earth, each proposing a law for the new lunar society. Every submission is reviewed, expanded in detail, and put to a public vote.

The host is alone up there. For now.
